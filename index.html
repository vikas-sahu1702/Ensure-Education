How Ensure Education Works

The transparent 4-stage mechanism protecting your degree tuition.

01

College Partnership & Admission

When you secure admission into a participating college, the admissions office introduces the Ensure Education scheme. The institution verifies that you are an actively matriculating student.

02

Simple Student Registration

You register on the platform using your College ID or Fee Receipt. Nominate one family member as your fee payer. Father/Mother nomination requires no income proof; the fee payer must be ≤ 55 years old.

03

Pay 1% Scheme Premium

The system dynamically computes exactly 1% of your course fee. For a ₹3,00,000 degree, you pay ₹3,000 once. You authenticate with 2FA and receive an instant Policy Card.

04

Active Education Protection

Your degree is protected for its entire normal duration. In the unfortunate event of the fee payer's demise, Ensure Education reviews the municipal death certificate and disburses remaining fees directly to the college.

Policy Document

Ensure Education Education Protection Policy

Scope of coverage, terms of validity, and legal protections.

Coverage Scope

The Ensure Education Education Protection Policy covers the remaining unpaid academic tuition fees of a registered student if their nominated fee payer suffers demise during the active policy term.

Coverage is limited to the course tuition fee officially registered during onboarding. Payouts are made directly to the partner college in which the student remains in good academic standing.

Standard Exclusions

  • • Demise occurring prior to the policy issue date and fee payment
  • • Misrepresentation of fee payer age (> 55 years at registration)
  • • Student expulsion or voluntary withdrawal from the degree course
  • • Non-academic auxiliary expenses (e.g. personal hostel luxury amenities, travel) unless explicitly covered in total fee schedule

Terms & Conditions

Operating guidelines and policy provisions for the Ensure Education Platform.

1. Eligibility & Registration

Registration is restricted to enrolled students of partner colleges. The student must upload valid college proof and nominate an eligible fee payer.

2. Fee Payer & Age Limit

The fee payer nominated by the student must be ≤ 55 years of age on the date of registration. Submissions exceeding this threshold are null and void.

3. Income Documentation

Father or Mother nominations are exempt from income documentation. Nominations of other eligible relatives require verifiable income proof.

4. Premium Calculation

Scheme premium is fixed at exactly 1.00% of the total verified course fee. College incentives (5%) are separate partner considerations.

5. Claims & Settlement

In the event of fee payer demise, claim filings require municipal death certificate verification. Approved benefits are settled directly with the partner college.
